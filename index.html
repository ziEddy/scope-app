Scope

A personal assistant program that one person runs on their own Mac · 한 사람이 자기 맥에서 쓰는 개인 도구

What it is

Scope answers its owner's own questions, asked in the owner's private Slack channel, by searching the owner's own notes and, once connected, the owner's own Google Drive. It is not offered to anyone else, has no sign-up, and has no other users.

무엇인가

Scope는 주인이 자기 Slack 채널에 올린 질문에, 주인의 노트와 (연결했을 때) 주인의 Google Drive를 찾아 답하는 개인 프로그램입니다. 다른 사람에게 제공하지 않고, 가입도 다른 사용자도 없습니다.

Google data

Scope asks Google only for read-only access to Google Drive (drive.readonly). How that data is handled is described in the privacy policy.
